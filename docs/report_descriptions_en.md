# BDI Report Descriptions (English)

Free translation of [`report_descriptions_pt.md`](report_descriptions_pt.md).
Organized by the same section structure as [`reports_catalog.md`](reports_catalog.md).
Portuguese names are shown in parentheses for cross-reference with the catalog.

## Fixed Income

- **OTC instrument list** *(Cadastro de instrumentos)* — Information on financial instruments traded on the exchange and available for trading on B3's OTC (Balcão) market.
- **Repo Debenture Transactions** *(Debêntures negociações compromissadas)* — Information on repo transactions involving debentures registered on a platform or system such as B3.
- **DI over** — The interbank overnight deposit rate; a key short-term interest rate benchmark in Brazil.
- **OTC position** *(Estoque)* — Consolidated information on the volumes held in the registration and depository platform.
- **Settlement** *(Liquidação)* — Information on settlement volumes — the process of completing a financial transaction, namely the transfer of assets and corresponding payment between the parties of a securities trade.
- **Consolidated negotiation** *(Negociação consolidada)* — Formerly the "Registros consolidados" table under the OTC chapter. Shows the quantity and financial volume of fixed-income instruments registered on a specific platform or system.
- **Repurchase dealings** *(Negociações compromissadas)* — Information on repo transactions registered on a platform or system such as B3.
- **Fixed income tick by tick** *(Negócio a negócio)* — Individual, trade-by-trade transactions, reported separately rather than aggregated. Updated every 15 minutes, covering trades on the trading platform (debentures, CRAs, CRIs, closed-end fund shares) and the registration platform (CDCA, COE, financial notes, LIG, commercial notes). B3 may apply adjustments on T+1.
- **Fixed income securities trades conducted on Puma** *(Negócios de VM de renda fixa realizados no Puma)* — Fixed-income trades executed on the Puma platform: debentures, CRI, CRA, FIDC, and fixed-income ETFs, with trading and offers settled on T+0 and T+1.
- **Register** *(Registro)* — Quantity and financial volume of fixed-income instruments registered on a specific platform or system.
- **TDA - Nominal values** *(TDA - Valores nominais)* — Published on the fifth business day of the month; covers Agrarian Debt Bonds (TDA), instruments financing agricultural production in Brazil, issued by financial institutions and backed by rural producers' debts.

## Equities — Stock Summary

- **Equities** *(Ações)* — Daily trading averages.
- **Equities - transactions summary** *(Ações - resumo das operações)* — Information on transactions carried out by listed companies over a given period, with value and share data.
- **Instruments consolidated** *(Cadastro de instrumentos)* — Details of the different available financial instruments, including characteristics, specifications, and trading rules.
- **Margin scenarios for liquid assets** *(Cenários de margem para ativos líquidos)* — Margin requirements for trading liquid assets under different scenarios, illustrating how price movements affect the margin required.
- **Indicative optimized portfolio value (IOPV) ETFs** *(Comportamento dos valores de referência das cotas dos ETFs)* — Tracks the evolution of the Indicative Optimized Portfolio Value (IOPV) of ETF shares over time.
- **Forward market** *(Mercado a termo)* — Detailed view of forward-market operations, where investors buy or sell assets with payment and delivery scheduled for a future date.
- **Daily averages - annual volume (BRL million)** *(Médias diárias – volume em um ano)* — Average transaction data for equities over a one-year period.
- **Trading strategies** *(Negociação de estratégias)* — Analysis of trading approaches used in the market, including buy/sell strategies, asset allocation, and risk management.
- **Consolidated trades of the session** *(Negócios consolidados do pregão)* — Summary of a trading day's operations: volumes, values, and main assets traded.
- **Consolidated trades of the non-regular session** *(Negócios consolidados do pregão não regular)* — Summary of non-standard transactions carried out in a session.
- **Tick by tick** *(Negócio a negócio)* — Individual, real-time transactions (price, volume, timestamp) — this is the report covered by this project's `tick_by_tick` pipeline.

## Equities — Indicators and Bulletins

- **Composition of indices' portfolios** *(Composição das carteiras dos índices)* — Distribution and quantity of equities making up the main market indices, including theoretical quantity and relative weight for the current portfolio cycle.
- **Conilon coffee price formation centers** *(Distribuição dos locais de formação de lotes café Conilon)* — Geographic overview of Conilon coffee lot locations, useful for analyzing key production and supply areas.
- **Type 4/5 coffee price formation centers** *(Distribuição dos locais de formação de lotes café tipo 4/5)* — Same geographic overview, for type 4/5 coffee lots.
- **Crop statistics - approved arabica coffee 4/5** *(Estatísticas de safras - café Arábica 4/5)* — Harvest statistics for certified and approved Arabica coffee lots.
- **Crop statistics - approved Conilon coffee** *(Estatísticas de safras - café Conilon)* — Harvest statistics for certified and approved Conilon coffee lots.
- **Indexes evolution** *(Evolução dos índices)* — Trajectory and changes in the main market indices over time.
- **Exchange rates history (Resolution BCB no. 120)** *(Histórico de taxas de câmbio)* — Official Central Bank exchange rates for the last 5 complete years plus the current year.
- **Economic indicators** *(Indicadores econômicos)* — Indicators used to price futures and options contracts, published daily with same-day (T+0) values, in CSV.
- **Updates** *(Informativos)* — *(no description available)*
- **Valid lots for contract settlement - arabica coffee** *(Lotes válidos para liquidação - café Arábica)* — Arabica coffee lots eligible for contract settlement, meeting quality and quantity criteria.
- **Valid lots for contract settlement - Conilon coffee** *(Lotes válidos para liquidação - café Conilon)* — Same criteria, for Conilon coffee.
- **Participation of investors** *(Participação dos investidores)* — Contribution of different investor profiles to B3's total trading volume.
- **Participation of investors monthly** *(Participação dos investidores mensal)* — Cumulative totals (buys + sells) by market type, for the prior month.
- **Preview of indices' theoretical portfolios** *(Prévia das carteiras teóricas de índices)* — B3 publishes three previews of new index compositions across each cycle. *No report is currently published for download.*

## Equities — Biggest Movers

- **IBOVESPA equities - largest gains** *(Ações do IBOVESPA - maiores altas)* — IBOVESPA constituents with the largest percentage gains over the period.
- **IBOVESPA equities - largest losses** *(Ações do IBOVESPA - maiores baixas)* — IBOVESPA constituents with the largest price declines over the period.
- **Most traded equities - forward** *(Ações mais negociadas - a termo)* — Equities with the highest forward-contract trading volume.
- **Most traded equities - call options** *(Opções mais negociadas - opções de compra)* — Most actively traded call options.
- **Most traded equities - put options** *(Opções mais negociadas - opções de venda)* — Most actively traded put options.
- **Most traded equities - cash** *(Ações mais negociadas - à vista)* — Equities with the highest cash-market trading volume.
- **Cash market - largest gains** *(Mercado à vista - maiores altas)* — Largest cash-market price gains.
- **Cash market - largest losses** *(Mercado à vista - maiores baixas)* — Largest cash-market price declines over the period.

## Derivatives — Summary

- **Derivatives** *(Derivativos)* — Daily averages of contracts traded.
- **Derivatives - mark to market** *(Derivativos - mark to market)* — Current valuation of derivatives positions, reflecting market value.
- **Derivatives - transactions summary** *(Derivativos - resumo das operações)* — Summary of derivatives transactions: volumes and prices over a given period.

## Derivatives — OTC Derivatives

- **Inventory with CCP** *(Estoque com CCP)* — Transactions settled through a Central Counterparty Clearing House (CCP) acting as intermediary.
- **Inventory without CCP** *(Estoque sem CCP)* — Transactions carried out directly between parties, without CCP intermediation.
- **Flexible options** *(Opções flexíveis)* — Flexible fixed-income derivative options: characteristics and volumes traded.
- **Record with CCP** *(Registro com CCP)* — Fixed-income derivatives transactions registered with the CCP, including volumes.
- **Record without CCP** *(Registro sem CCP)* — Fixed-income derivatives transactions outside CCP registration.
- **Swap** — Fixed-income swap contracts: volumes and transaction types.
- **Electronic term** *(Termo eletrônico)* — Electronic fixed-income forward contracts. *No download currently available (tested across multiple dates).*

## Derivatives — Exchange-Traded Derivatives

- **Instruments consolidated** *(Cadastro de instrumentos)* — All available equity derivatives, with characteristics and specifications.
- **Tick by tick** *(Negócio a negócio)* — Individual derivatives transactions: price, volume, timestamp — covered by this project's `tick_by_tick` pipeline.
- **Consolidated trades of the session** *(Negócios consolidados do pregão)* — Derivatives transactions during a session: volumes and values.
- **Consolidated trades of the non-regular session** *(Negócios consolidados do pregão não regular)* — Non-regular derivatives transactions.
- **Open positions** *(Posições em aberto)* — Volume and value of open equity-derivatives positions.
- *Analytical open positions overview* *(Quadro analítico das posições em aberto)* — mentioned as an existing report, but has no corresponding catalog entry yet (not implemented).

## Other Data — Clearing and Depository

- **Equities in custody - ADR program** *(Ações custodiadas - programa de ADR)* — Equities held in custody by a financial institution on behalf of investors.
- **Earnings credited** *(Crédito de proventos)* — Amounts received by investors as returns on equity or other equity-market investments.
- **Fungible custody** *(Custódia fungível)* — Assets held in custody that don't belong to the investor; ensures transparency and traceability over third-party asset management.
- **Period to deposit securities** *(Prazo para depósito de títulos)* — Deadlines for depositing securities with the Custody department, for holders entitled to earnings.

## Other Data — Securities Lending

- **Registered securities lending** *(Empréstimos registrados)* — Detailed information on formally registered securities loans; each asset's average rate is the trade-volume-weighted average for the session date (or the last available calculated rate, if no trading occurred). This is the report used in `examples/read_single_bdi_report.py`.
- **Trades** *(Negócios)* — *(no description available)*
- **Open positions** *(Posições em aberto)* — Securities loans still active and not yet returned — lets investors monitor ongoing lending exposure.
- **Renewals** *(Renovações)* — Daily loan renewal transactions, including interest rate and term.

## Other Data — COE (Structured Notes)

- **Inventory** *(Estoque)* — Quantity and value of COEs (structured operation certificates) currently available for trading.
- **Records** *(Registro)* — COEs registered with B3, including issuance details, characteristics, conditions, and dates.
